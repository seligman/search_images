# Sample Image Credits

These 19 images are provided for demonstrating the image database.
Every image is under a Creative Commons license that permits free
reuse and redistribution. Images under CC BY require attribution; the
table below provides it. CC0 images are released into the public domain.

Images 01 to 18 were collected through the Openverse API
(https://openverse.org) and resized for the demo. Image 19, a 360-degree
panorama, comes from Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org);
it was resized from 5760x2880 to 4096x2048 pixels and is otherwise
unchanged. Resizing is permitted by all licenses listed.

The capture dates stored in images 01 to 18 were added for the demo's
timeline and are not when those photos were taken; image 19's date is
the photographer's own.

The locations of nine of images 01 to 18 were added from their source
pages (read in October 2026; see "Locations" below) and are only as
precise as those pages; image 19's GPS position is the photographer's
own. Only the metadata changed: the pictures themselves are the same,
and no GPS time was added, so the made-up dates stay as they are.

## Locations

Where a source page gives the photo's position -- a Flickr geotag -- the
picture holds it as EXIF GPS, with its accuracy in GPSHPositioningError:
Flickr records the map zoom level a location was set at (16 street
level, 15 and 14 a little coarser), taken here as about 100, 200 and 400
metres. A geotag may have been placed on Flickr's map by hand rather
than measured by a GPS. Image 19 holds its camera's own GPS position
(also its camera location on Wikimedia Commons), with no stated
accuracy.

Where the page names only a town, a region or a continent, the picture
holds only those names, in its XMP location fields (City, State, Country
and the country code), and no position: the place is shown and found,
but has no pin on the map, as a pin at the town's centre would look more
exact than the page is. The names are the page's own; for 04 ("Alnmouth,
Northumberland, UK") the region and the country are named as the
project's place names (GeoNames) name them: England, United Kingdom.

Image 11 holds both: its geotag, and in its XMP location fields the
names of the page's location line (Cimadera, Ticino, Switzerland). It
was taken a few kilometres from the Italian border, where the nearest
town the place names list is Italian; a file that names its place is
shown by those names, so it reads as its page does.
